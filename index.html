Featured Project

CFPB Consumer Complaint Analysis | 2024–2025

An analysis of 137,016 unique consumer complaint records involving checking and savings accounts.

The project investigates an unusual increase in complaints during January 2025, identifies the financial institutions contributing to the spike, and examines differences in reported issues.

Key finding: Navy Federal Credit Union and Capital One together accounted for 66.3% of January 2025 complaints in the analyzed dataset. Their dominant reported issues differed, indicating distinct questions for further investigation—not confirmed underlying causes.

Tools: Python, SQL, Pandas, Tableau Public
